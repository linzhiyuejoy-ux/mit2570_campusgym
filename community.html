Mini Challenges

Social Steps Challenge

  • Pick a daily step goal: 6k / 8k / 10k.
  • Track with any step app and screenshot your steps.
  • Share progress in the community group 5 days this week.
  • Bonus: take a 10–15 min campus loop between classes.

Desk–Stretch Bingo

  • Between classes: cat–cow, neck rolls, doorway pec stretch (quick 1–2 min).
  • Mark a bingo square each time you stretch — aim for any 4-in-a-row this week.
  • Use a 25/5 study timer and stretch during the 5-min breaks.
  • Pre-exam reset: 30s box breathing + shoulder rolls to clear the stress.

Join now

5-Day Budget Lunchbox

  • Pack one cheap, balanced lunch each weekday.
  • Use staples: rice/pasta, frozen veg, eggs/chickpeas/tuna + a simple sauce.
  • Aim for less than $5 per meal; cook once and make 2 portions.
  • Post a photo + cost in the stories page for accountability.
