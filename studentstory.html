How students make it work

Everyone here started simple：10–15 minutes, a dorm-friendly circuit, or one cheaper meal a day. Try one idea that fits your week, then build from there.

 

“I used to scroll between classes. Now I run the 7-move no-equipment circuit in an empty study room. It takes 12 minutes and wakes my brain up.”

— Lina (first-year student)

What Lina used from Campus Fit

  • No-Equipment: Leg circuit (squats, lunges, step-ups, skaters).
  • Yoga & Stretch mini flow for cool-down.
  • $25–30 Week Plan for cheap lunches.

Lina’s quick routine

  • 3 rounds: 15 squats · 12 forward-to-reverse lunges/side · 20s wall sit.
  • Finish with 30s cat–cow + 30s forward fold.
  • Whole thing: ~12–14 minutes between classes.

After 4 weeks

  • More energy in 2 p.m. lectures.
  • Saved money by packing one meal/day.
  • Confidence boost from small daily wins.
 

“Lab runs long, so I book 15-minute slots and do a campus gym push–pull mashup. Tracking sets keeps me honest.”

— Marcus (Grad student)

What Marcus used from Campus Fit

  • Campus Gym: 15-Minute Push–Pull (DB bench ↔ one-arm row; shoulder press ↔ lat pulldown).
  • Quick Healthy Meals (chicken noodle soup, omelette, salmon).
  • Posture reset (box breathing + seated fold).

Marcus’s 15-minute plan

  • 3 rounds: dumbbell bench 8–10 + one-arm row 8–10/side
  • Shoulder press 10 + lat pulldown 10
  • Face pulls 12–15 + dead bug 8/side
  • Finish: 30s wall stretch, slow breaths

After 6 weeks

  • Neck/upper-back tension down.
  • Form feels cleaner; weights inch up slowly.
  • Dinner is on autopilot with two quick recipes.
 

“I kept skipping dinner. Following the $25–30 budget plan fixed that. I pack one meal a day in a reusable container—way less stress during midterms.”

— Sam (Residence student) — Sam (Residence student)

What Sam used from Campus Fit

  • Staples list + 3 day menu (oats, tuna pasta, chickpea curry).
  • No-equipment finisher 2 times/week for mood.

Sam’s easy system

  • Sunday: cook chickpea curry + rice (2 nights of dinners).
  • Weekdays: alternate tuna pasta/egg and cabbage rice.
  • Always carry a container & fork; reheat at library microwaves.

After 3 weeks

  • Regular meals → better focus at night study sessions.
  • Groceries under budget; less takeout.
