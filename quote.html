Quote of the Day

Small steps, kept daily, become strong.
— Campus Fit

60-Second Reset

  • Box breathing 4–4–6 * 4 cycles（inhale-hold-exhale).
  • Posture check：feet flat · ribcage stacked · relax jaw/shoulders.
  • One tiny next step：write 1 actionable task & start 2 minutes.

2-Minute Desk Release

  • Neck: look-left/right * 6；chin-to-chest * 6.
  • Shoulders: scap pulls × 10；arm circles * 8.
  • Wrists: flex/extend * 10；shake-out 10s.

Focus Reboot (3 steps)

  • Environment：timer 20 min · clear 1 item from desk.
  • Attention：close extra tabs · phone to Do-Not-Disturb.
  • Compassion：tell yourself “Progress over perfection.”
