Start here: Campus eating, simplified

  • The 1–1–1 plate: one protein (eggs, tuna, tofu) + one base (rice, pasta, bread) + one veg/fruit (frozen mix, tomatoes, apples) — add a simple flavor (soy sauce, garlic/onion powder, chili).
  • 90-second wins: mug omelette, yogurt + fruit + oats, or chicken-noodle soup boosted with frozen veg.
  • Shop once, stretch it: small haul = rice/pasta, eggs, frozen veg, canned tomatoes/beans/tuna, yogurt, bananas/apples.
  • Pack & reheat: keep a reusable container; most libraries & student centres have microwaves and lounge seating.
